# TajikSpoken AI

An open-source dataset for conversational Tajik language and artificial intelligence training.

## Mission

Our mission is to help AI models understand and communicate in natural, everyday Tajik, including informal speech, local dialects, abbreviations, and Russian-Tajik mixed speech.

## Examples

| Standard Tajik | Spoken Tajik |
|---|---|
| Ман | Ма |
| Чаро | Чиба |
| Ҳозир | Ози |
| Ба ман бигӯ | Мада бугу |
| Ҳамааш хуб мешавад | Хамаш хуб меша |

## Dataset

- dictionary.json — conversational vocabulary
- sentences.jsonl — parallel sentence dataset

## Roadmap

## Contributions

Native Tajik speakers are welcome to contribute.

This dataset is in early development and currently represents a limited sample of conversational Tajik.

## Our Goal

Make artificial intelligence accessible to everyone who speaks Tajik naturally.
